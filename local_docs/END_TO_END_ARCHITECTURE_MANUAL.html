3. Hardware Fingerprinting & Sybil Defense Engine

To prevent malicious users from bypassing search limits and VTON trials by switching to Incognito mode or rotating VPN IP addresses, ShopSmart AI deploys an invisible client-side Hardware Footprint Engine (src/lib/deviceFingerprint.js).

Figure 3.1: Hardware & Sub-Pixel Canvas Fingerprint Pipeline Engine: src/lib/deviceFingerprint.js

🎨 Sub-Pixel Canvas 2D Entropy

Different operating systems, graphic cards, and font rasterizers render emojis (🐶🛍️) and bezier curves with minuscule sub-pixel differences. This creates a distinct visual entropy without recording any PII.

🎮 WebGL GPU Hardware Extraction

Extracts the unmasked graphic card vendor (e.g., ANGLE (Apple, Apple M2 Max, OpenGL 4.1) or NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080). Virtual machines and automated headless bots typically expose SwiftShader or Mesa Offscreen.

🔊 AudioContext Oscillator Synthesis

Synthesizes an inaudible audio buffer with a DynamicsCompressorNode. The float32 audio output varies across hardware soundcards and mathematical floating-point implementations.

🛡️ 100% Privacy & DPDP 2023 Compliant

No cookies, MAC addresses, or personal names are ever gathered. The hash is completely one-way (SHA-256) and cannot be reversed into user identity.
